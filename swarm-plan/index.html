Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. On the sign-in screen you can choose Use without an account and the app runs entirely on your device — no sign-up, no internet connection required. Signing in with Apple or Google adds cloud sync across your devices.

How do I unlock coach features?

Playbook editing, roster, schedule, practice and stats are coach features. After signing in, enter the coach passcode for your team or organization on the role screen. If you don't have one, contact whoever set up your team, or email us.

How do I join as a player?

Your coach adds you to the roster using your email address. Sign in with that same email and the app links you to your coach's team automatically.

My data isn't syncing between devices

Cloud sync only runs when you're signed in. Check that you're signed in with the same Apple or Google account on both devices, and that you have an internet connection. If it still doesn't sync, sign out and sign back in.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete Account. This removes your account and profile. If you'd also like your team data permanently erased from our servers, email us from your account address and we'll delete it within 30 days.

I forgot which account I used

Email us and we'll help you identify it. Note that Sign in with Apple may have created a private relay address on your behalf — check your Apple ID settings under Sign in with Apple.

Report a bug or request a feature

Email us with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots help a lot. We read every message.
